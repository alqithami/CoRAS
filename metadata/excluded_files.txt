The supplementary archive intentionally excludes raw public datasets, generated large NPZ datasets, model checkpoints, AutoEval trajectory pickle files, and full prediction-set dumps. These artifacts are regenerated by the provided scripts from public dataset identifiers and fixed configuration files.

Excluded categories:
- data/*.npz larger than small smoke-test examples
- *.pt, *.pth, *.ckpt model checkpoints
- raw DROID / PushT / AutoEval data
- AutoEval trajectory pickle files
- large prediction_sets_*.csv dumps not needed for reported aggregate tables
